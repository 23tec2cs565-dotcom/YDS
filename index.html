Comprehensive Turnkey Interior & Architectural Services in Jaipur

From initial space planning and photorealistic 3D rendering to meticulous civil construction, custom carpentry, and on-site handover, Younick Design Studio manages the entire project lifecycle with uncompromising transparency and craftsmanship.

1. Luxury Residential Interior Design

Bespoke home interiors designed for modern living. We specialize in luxury living room styling, master bedrooms, modular kitchens with German hardware, custom fluted panelling, acoustic partitions, automated ambient 3000K warm lighting layouts, and curated Italian marble integration.

2. Commercial & Corporate Architecture

Transforming brand vision into memorable spatial environments. Our commercial portfolio spans retail boutiques, jewellery showrooms with high-security display vitrines, boutique fitness centers, corporate executive offices, wellness clinics, and specialized healthcare facilities.

3. Turnkey Civil Construction & Execution

Full-scale civil construction with total material and labor management. We handle foundation-to-finish masonry, reinforced concrete structures, structural wall modifications, plumbing grids, electrical MEP planning, and strict quality control without third-party contractor delays.

4. Full-Scale Home & Structural Renovation

Metamorphosis of aging properties and bare shells into contemporary architectural masterpieces. Services include structural re-engineering, waterproofing, floor leveling, bespoke cabinetry upgrades, modern false ceilings, and acoustic soundproofing.

5. 3D Architectural Visualization & Walkthroughs

Experience your space in photorealistic detail before construction begins. We generate millimeter-accurate 3D walkthroughs, lighting simulations, material mood boards, and CAD working drawings to eliminate ambiguity and prevent costly on-site revisions.

6. Spatial Design & Budget Consultation

Professional architectural advisory covering floor layout optimization, detailed material procurement estimates, Vastu-aligned zoning, and milestone-based project budget scheduling.
