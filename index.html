Our Structured 5-Step Architectural Design Process

We follow a rigorous five-stage methodology to ensure every residential and commercial project is delivered on time, within budget, and with mastercrafted quality:

  1. Discovery & Space Consultation: On-site laser measurement, lifestyle requirements assessment, and architectural feasibility study.
  2. Spatial Layouts & Mood Boards: 2D zoning, furniture layouts, circulation pathways, and curated material color palettes.
  3. High-Fidelity 3D Visualizations: Photorealistic interior and exterior renderings showing lighting, custom woodwork, and stone textures.
  4. BOQ Estimation & Material Procurement: Transparent itemized budgeting, factory-crafted joinery, and direct material sourcing from certified vendors.
  5. Turnkey Site Execution & Handover: Meticulous contractor management, quality checks, deep cleaning, and defect liability warranty handover.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does interior design cost in Jaipur?

Turnkey residential interior design in Jaipur typically ranges from ₹1,200 to ₹2,500+ per sq. ft. for premium homes (including custom woodwork, modular kitchen, false ceilings, lighting, and premium paint). Luxury villa executions scale based on Italian marble, automation, and bespoke furniture.

What are the advantages of turnkey interior execution?

Turnkey execution provides single-point responsibility where Younick Design Studio handles design, 3D visualization, material procurement, carpentry, civil work, electrical, plumbing, and site management with guaranteed timelines and transparent pricing.

Do you undertake interior projects outside Jaipur?

Yes, while our headquarters is located in Civil Lines, Jaipur, we regularly execute projects across Sikar, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, and all of Rajasthan, as well as providing pan-India 3D visualization and design consultation.
